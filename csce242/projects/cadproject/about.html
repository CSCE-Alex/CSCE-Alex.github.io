Our Heritage

The Pursuit of Optical Perfection

Founded in 2024 by Alex Perez, Carolina Auto Details was born out of complete frustration with assembly-line car washes that permanently damage delicate clear coats. Alex envisioned a sterile, laboratory-like detailing environment where chemistry and optical physics are applied to bring automotive paint to its highest possible aesthetic potential.

Today, our Columbia studio houses clean-room lighting arches, paint depth detail, and specialized compounds sourced from around the world. We treat every vehicle as a rolling masterpiece.
