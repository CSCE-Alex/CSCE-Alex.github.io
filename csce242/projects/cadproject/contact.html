Common Inquiries

Automotive Detailing FAQs

Got questions before configuring your appointment? Read through our technical protocols below.

How long does a paint correction run actually require?

Depending on clear coat hardness and depth of defect, multi-stage correction requires 24 to 48 hours of meticulously recorded polishing passes to eliminate swirls entirely.

What is the key advantage of Gyeon Quartz Ceramic Coatings?

Gyeon Quartz chemical compounds bind directly to the clear coat at a molecular level, establishing a glass-like shell that guards against acid rain, UV oxidation, and paint etching.

Do you offer pick-up and delivery options?

Yes. We offer covered flatbed concierge vehicle transport across the greater Charlotte region for ultimate security and paint peace-of-mind.
